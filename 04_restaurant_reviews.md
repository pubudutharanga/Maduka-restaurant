### 12. Andreanna W — Written April 13, 2025
* **Visit**: April 2025 • Couples (21 contributions, 1 helpful vote)
* **Headline**: *Outstanding and humbling experience for both food and dolphin tour.*
* **Review**: "Stumbled across this place on the way to our Homestay in Kalpitiya. Maduka not only runs the most underrated restaurant in Sri Lanka he organises private tours to the spinner dolphins. (Cheapest tour I found). In addition to his generosity he sorted us out a Homestay for the night before the tour at Dolphin Chill which is a few metres away from his restaurant/dolphin tours. Which I would also recommend, it was vacant on the night we went to dinner the first time, if you were caught in a pinch or wanted to drink if you were driving. We went there on three occasions to eat, two for dinner and one for breakfast. All meals were outstanding, the dinners involved several vegetable curries and you can have fish and chicken on request, he normally has access to fish and squid/shellfish quite readily as his Dad fishes, so it is freshly caught. The highlights for me were the potato, pumpkin, pineapple curries and the coconut sambal. (The coconut sambal was served in the morning for breakfast). However they are all out of this world and you would think you were in a 5 star resort. When we went for the meals and for the tours, he offered us an abundance of coffee, water and ginger tea for free mostly. The other things to try would be the homemade cocktails which are made by his Dad, who ferments the fruit and ingredients that go into it himself. And the buffalo curd and coconut honey dessert. In addition to this, the second night when we went for dinner he set the table with flowers and incense making it a very romantic setting. Please do try this place when in the area, there is parking for bikes and tuks to the side of his restaurant. We drove a tuk there through Tuk tuk rentals.com. We were gutted we had to leave as we were treated like kings and felt like one of his family. If you would like to eat at a certain time please inform him via what’s app on number +94 729614412. Otherwise you could wait a while as they cook all their curries from scratch. On our second night we went to check in at the place he sorted for us, he had started to cook some of them, at 2pm. Please keep updated on google and latest reviews as he is in the process of having two rooms built for a small Homestay which we have asked to come back when he has built them, these should be ready for November/December 2025."
