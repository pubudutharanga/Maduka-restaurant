# Maduka Restaurant — Complete kitchen Section & Photo Specification

This document provides the complete content, photo-to-caption mapping, dish breakdown, and structured TypeScript data required to build the **Meal & Culinary Showcase Section** of the **Maduka Restaurant & Rooms** Next.js website.

## 1. Meal Section Key Highlights & Operational Details

* **Opening Hours & Service**: Open daily for **Breakfast, Brunch, Lunch, Dinner, and Beachside Drinks**.
* **Cooking Philosophy**: 100% home-cooked from scratch every day by **Nissansala** and **Maduka** using locally grown vegetables, freshly ground Sri Lankan spices, traditional clay pots (*walang*), and daily ocean/lagoon catch landed by **Maduka's father** and local fishermen right in front of the restaurant.
* **Important Guest Pre-Order Notice (UI Callout)**:
  > *"Because every curry and seafood dish is cooked fresh from scratch starting as early as 2:00 PM, we recommend pre-ordering your dinner or crab curry via WhatsApp at **+94 72 961 4412** so your table is ready right at sunset."*
* **Dietary Inclusivity**: Extensive **Vegetarian** and **Vegan** options available daily (all vegetable curries are prepared fresh with coconut milk and local herbs/spices).
* **Dining Atmosphere Options**:
  * **Private Beachfront Dining**: Tables set directly on the sand overlooking the Indian Ocean and returning fishing boats.
  * **Romantic Evening Setup**: Custom table settings with fresh tropical flowers and incense upon request.
  * **Shaded Thatch Cabin & Hammock Lounge**: Cool, breezy under-roof dining for sunny afternoons or light rain, plus beachside hammocks while meals are prepared.
* **Payment at Restaurant**: Accepts both **Credit Cards** and **Cash**.

Use these 5 menu tabs in the Next.js **Meal Section** component (`src/components/MenuShowcase.tsx`), combining the photo items above with guest-favorite dishes from the reviews:

  * **Aubergine (Brinjal / Eggplant) Curry**
  * **Fresh Green Bean Curry**
  * **Spiced Beetroot Curry**
  * **Sliced Cabbage & Cauliflower Mallung/Curry**
  * **Accompaniments**: Steamed white rice or fragrant garlic/yellow rice, crispy *papadums* (popadoms), and homemade *pol sambol* (coconut sambal).

### Tab 2: Fresh Ocean & Puttalam Lagoon Seafood
* **Featured Photos**: `1792856947711.jpg` (Maduka Special Crab Curry), `1792856879249.jpg` (Grilled Daily Catch with Tropical Salad & Roast Potatoes), plus optional `1792856921212.jpg` (Mixed Grilled Prawns, Crab & Fish Platter)
* **Included Dishes**:
  * **Maduka Special Crab Curry**: Freshly caught Puttalam Lagoon crabs cooked to order in traditional clay-pot curry gravy.
  * **Grilled Catch of the Day**: Reef or ocean fish landed right in front of the restaurant, grilled to perfection and served with vibrant tropical salad, yellow/garlic rice, and golden roast potatoes.
  * **Grilled Jumbo Tiger Prawns & Prawn Curry**: Freshly landed local prawns either char-grilled or simmered in coconut spice gravy.
  * **Fresh Squid / Calamari & Shellfish**: Available daily on request based on the morning and evening fishing boat catch.

### Tab 3: Breakfast on the Beach
* **Featured Photos**: `1792856967569.jpg` (Sri Lankan Breakfast Spread), `1792856798714.jpg` (Tropical Fresh Fruit Platter)
* **Included Dishes**:
  * **Sri Lankan Breakfast Favourites**: Crispy bowl-shaped hoppers (*appa*), yellow egg curry, spicy *lunu miris* sambol, fresh morning *pol sambol* (coconut sambal), savory lentil *vadai*, rolled crepes/pancakes, and sweet coconut rolls.
  * **Tropical Fresh Fruit Platter**: Sliced watermelon, papaya, pineapple, local bananas, guava, and fresh lime.
  * **Morning Beverages**: Newly introduced **Espresso Coffee**, freshly squeezed tropical fruit juices, Ceylon tea, and ginger tea.

### Tab 4: Desserts & Sweet Treats
* **Included Dishes**:
  * **Traditional Buffalo Curd & Coconut Honey (Kithul Treacle)**: Classic Sri Lankan coastal dessert praised by guests.
  * **Sweet Coconut Pancake Rolls (*Pani Pol*)**: Served during breakfast or with afternoon tea.

### Tab 5: Beach Bar, Homemade Cocktails & Refreshments
* **Featured Photo**: `1792856850719.jpg` (Ice-Cold Beer in Beach Ice Bucket)
* **Included Drinks**:
  * **Maduka's Father's Homemade Cocktails**: Unique house cocktails crafted from fruit and local ingredients fermented in-house by Maduka's dad.
  * **Ice-Cold Local Beers**: Served in ice buckets right on the sand or at sunset while watching the fishing boats return.
  * **Warm Spiced Ginger Tea, Espresso Coffee & Fresh Fruit Juices**: Served throughout the day (welcome fruit juice upon arrival; complimentary ginger tea, coffee, and water often shared with tour and dining guests).
