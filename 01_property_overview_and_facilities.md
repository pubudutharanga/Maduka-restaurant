## 2. Host & Neighborhood Profile

* **About the Property**: Set in a tranquil beachfront location between Puttalam and Kalpitiya with direct access to a private beach and ocean views. Built as a peaceful, brand-new (2 rooms completed around November/December 2025), budget-friendly coastal escape featuring a terrace, private beach area, and lush garden.
* **About the Hosts**: Maduka and his wife, Nissansala, have professional backgrounds in hospitality and cooking and speak good English. Maduka's fishing family owns the land; his father catches fresh fish and drives the boat for private spinner dolphin tours, and also ferments fruit and ingredients to make homemade cocktails.
* **Neighborhood Vibe**: Located in a quiet, largely undeveloped traditional fishing town where daily life revolves around the sea. Guests can watch small fishing boats head out in the early morning and late evening and return with the fresh catch of the day. Note from guest reviews: it is a peaceful fisherman's beach with good swimming water, with a coal port to the south and wind turbines visible in the distance.

## 4. Food & Drink (Maduka Restaurant)

* **On-Site Restaurant**: Maduka Restaurant (Family-friendly & Traditional ambience).
* **Meals Served**: Breakfast (à la carte, included with select room options), Brunch, Lunch, Dinner, and Drinks.
* **Dietary Options**: Vegetarian and Vegan options widely available.
* **Signature Dishes & Drinks Mentioned by Guests**:
  * **Seafood**: Freshly caught fish of the day, Puttalam Lagoon crab curry, grilled prawns, and squid/shellfish (landed directly in front of the restaurant by Maduka's father and local fishermen).
  * **Sri Lankan Rice & Curry**: Traditional medley including potato curry, pumpkin curry, sweet-and-sour pineapple curry, aubergine (eggplant) curry, daal, green beans, sliced cabbage, garlic rice, salad, and popadoms.
  * **Breakfast & Dessert**: Coconut sambal (served at breakfast), fresh fruit juice on arrival, and buffalo curd with coconut honey dessert.
  * **Beverages**: Homemade fermented fruit cocktails (made by Maduka's father), cold beer, ginger tea, coffee, and complimentary water.
